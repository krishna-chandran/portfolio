Software engineer joins Agtonomy to scale farm-robot telemetry

Cincinnati  |  {{ .Date.Format "January 2006" }}  |  Agtech

Krishnakumar Chandran, a Cincinnati engineer trained in aerospace, has joined Agtonomy to work on vehicle telemetry. The brief is practical: pipelines that stay up when a fleet of autonomous farm machines starts talking all at once.

He comes from ThorDrive, where he led data engineering on an internal platform for ROS bags, live logs and labels at tractor scale, after a stint as perception engineer on lidar, thermal cameras and sensor fusion.
