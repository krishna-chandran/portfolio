Autonomy produces more tape than most companies are ready to keep: lidar, cameras, thermal, CAN, operator notes, the crash log from last Tuesday. A warehouse with a search box is not a desk.
<!--more-->

Eitri was an attempt at the desk. Collect the bag. Store it so it can be found. Clip it. Label it. Hand it to perception without a week of archaeology.

The lesson was not a particular database. It was that the data path *is* the product, once the vehicle leaves the parking lot. Telemetry at Agtonomy is the same argument in a different field.
