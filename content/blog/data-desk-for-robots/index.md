Autonomy produces more tape than most companies are ready to keep: lidar, cameras, thermal, CAN, operator notes, the crash log from last Tuesday. A warehouse with a search box is not enough.
<!--more-->

{{< well kind="lead" src="images/krishna-profile.jpg" prompt="Mug, 16:9 crop of the engineer at a desk, screens with waveforms not logos, Cincinnati." caption="The columnist, briefly. The argument is the bag file." />}}

Eitri was an attempt at that platform. Collect the bag. Store it so it can be found. Clip it. Label it. Hand it to perception without a week of archaeology.

The lesson was not a particular database. It was that the data path *is* the product, once the vehicle leaves the parking lot. At Agtonomy the same argument became three pipes — telemetry, value metrics and every stop — so a grower could see a return and an engineer could see a fault.
