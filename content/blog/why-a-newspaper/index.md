A career is already a paper of record. Dates, bylines, a few stories that deserved the front page, and a long inside section nobody reads unless they have to.
<!--more-->

This site is that paper. The masthead is a name. The columns are years. The jump lines go to the work itself — Agtonomy, ThorDrive, the Cincinnati lab, the UAV notes from 2017.

I am not trying to look like a product company. I am trying to make the timeline legible: what was the lead that year, and what was a brief.
