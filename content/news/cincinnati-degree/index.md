CINCINNATI — In December 2018 Krishnakumar Chandran completed the Master of Engineering in aerospace at the University of Cincinnati. The GPA was 3.5 on 4.0. The scholarship had been 50 percent. The IMS–Mazak internship had ended the same month.

The degree file is unmanned systems, dynamics, controls and AI, plus the industrial project that put a spindle score on a Chicago floor. What he took out of the building was not a job title. It was the habit of sitting with data that arrived dirty and a machine that had to keep running.

In 2019 he would place at IQ E-Pitch and return to TCS in Michigan. The dateline on most of the later paper is still this one.
