CHENNAI — In September 2014 Krishnakumar Chandran joined Tata Consultancy Services as a software developer. The product was ignio AIOps. The appointment ran through July 2017.
<!--more-->

{{< well kind="lead" prompt="Wide, 16:9, Chennai office night: cubicles, network diagrams on a whiteboard, a single desk lamp, documentary." caption="TCS, Chennai. Network automation and ignio service operations." credit="Staff" />}}

**The duties.** Write ignio scripts for network administration. Cover the devices a data center actually owns: L2 and L3 switches, firewalls, load balancers, F5 and Infoblox. Build ignio Service Operations so those boxes could be handled without a person walking the closet. Take on the quieter AI work — event triage, incident prediction, automatic resolution — when a ticket would otherwise wait.

**How it was done.** About 160 scripts automated some 90 percent of those admin tasks. The languages were Python, PowerShell, Groovy and bash. The estate was Windows and Linux. PostgreSQL held what needed holding. In 2016 the same product went to Nielsen Media India for NetBackup automation and backup analytics; about half of that shop’s manual reporting time went away.

The habit — observe the estate, automate the night list — is the one he later took to a spindle, a tractor and a farm.
