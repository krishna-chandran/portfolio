NEW DELHI — On 1 October 2014 the *Journal of Basic and Applied Engineering Research*, from Krishi Sanskriti Publications at JNU, published “Conceptual design and analysis of link for arresting slat actuator play in TEJAS aircraft.” The authors were Krishnakumar Chandran, Manikandan C and Niranjana S.
<!--more-->

{{< well kind="lead" prompt="Wide, 16:9: a bound journal issue on a desk, title page cropped so the hed is readable, documentary." caption="Oct. 1, 2014. The method, the analysis and the link, on the record." credit="Staff" />}}

The paper is the public file of the HAL project: why the slat actuator developed play after about 300 runs, the approximate equations, the redundant link, and the CATIA–ANSYS check. It was accepted after review. HAL’s engineering team had already taken the findings into later evaluation.

A journal paper is not a product launch. It is how a first-degree project leaves the college and enters the literature. The later work on ignio and tractors does not cite it. The habit does: write the argument down so someone else can use it.
