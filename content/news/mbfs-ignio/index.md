UNITED STATES — From 2019 to 2021 Krishnakumar Chandran sat with Mercedes-Benz Financial Services to deploy ignio AIOps in the company’s data center. MBFS is the exclusive financial engine behind Mercedes-Benz USA. The outcome on the file is automation of that estate: incident response taken from a person-first queue toward a machine-first delivery model.

He was then TCS’s product architect for ignio. The collaboration was the customer engagement the role required: hear the operational challenge, design the integration with servers, network devices and business applications, and stay while the product went live.

Belk, Toyota Motors North America and Astellas sat in the same years, across retail, automobiles and pharmaceuticals. The public claim for ignio’s footprint — more than 260 Fortune 500 and Global 2000 corporations — is the product’s, not a count of personal installs. The MBFS engagement is the one named in the career file as a complete walk from manual servicing to machine-first.
