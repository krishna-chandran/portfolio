FARMINGTON HILLS, Mich. — In July 2019 Krishnakumar Chandran rejoined Tata Consultancy Services as a product architect. The appointment ran through September 2021. The products were ignio AIOps and ignio AI Digital Workspace — and, in the family, ignio Butterfly. The brief was production deployment, not a slide.
<!--more-->

{{< well kind="lead" prompt="Wide, 16:9, night NOC: several monitors with calm dashboards, empty chair, coffee, no readable client names." caption="Lead the delivery. Stay through go-live." credit="Staff" />}}

**The duties.** Lead production deployment and delivery of ignio AIOps and Digital Workspace. Integrate the product with the tools a client already owned: ServiceNow, SolarWinds, AppDynamics, Office 365, Active Directory, email and CyberArk. On Veeva Vault, automate user access and put chat-based ticket creation in front of the request. Build a log stream on Apache Kafka, Elasticsearch, Logstash, Kibana and Beats.

**How it was done.** The Kafka–ELK path cut debugging time by 90 percent. ignio went out in high availability, Ansible-driven. Self-healing closed Linux, database and application alerts in seconds. Mercedes-Benz Financial Services, Belk, Toyota Motors North America and Astellas sat among the accounts. The ignio product’s footprint is more than 260 Fortune 500 and Global 2000 shops; his piece was the architect who sat with the client, and the network and Windows automation from the Chennai years.

[Digitate’s brochure](https://digitate.com/assets/2024/Digitate_ignio_AIOps_Brochure_6.0.pdf) and [TCS’s note on an industry award](https://www.tcs.com/who-we-are/newsroom/press-release/digitate-ignio-named-best-overall-ai-platform-at-ai-breakthrough-awards) are the product’s public clips, not a personal medal.
