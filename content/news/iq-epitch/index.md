CINCINNATI — In 2019 Krishnakumar Chandran placed as second runner-up at IQ E-Pitch, the startup elevator-pitch competition at the University of Cincinnati’s Carl H. Lindner College of Business. The idea was a sport-analytics company; the demonstration used AI to pull insight from a soccer match.
<!--more-->

The contest is open to the university’s pitchers. The award is not a national engineering medal. It is on the record because it shows the same person who sat controls and ignio could stand up and argue for an AI product in a room that keeps time with a clock.

The later work did not become a soccer startup. The later work became tractors. The column is here so the file is complete.
