INDIA — In 2016 Krishnakumar Chandran sat with the IT team at Nielsen Media India Private Limited to deploy ignio, including NetBackup automation and backup data analytics. Nielsen is the usual gold standard in that country for market research, television ratings and consumer intelligence. The outcome on the record is that about half of the manual reporting and IT-operations time went away.

He was then a software developer at TCS in Chennai. The collaboration was with a client estate, not a second employer. Nielsen’s IT team wanted ignio on backup operations that had been a person and a spreadsheet — NetBackup automation and backup analytics — and a board instead of a hunt. About 50 percent of the time spent on manual reporting and IT operations went away.

The significance is not a press release. It is an outside shop, the usual gold standard in that country for ratings and consumer intelligence, that used the product and cut a measurable slice of night work. Network and Windows modules from the Chennai line were already the sort of thing a ratings house could run without rewriting the data center.
