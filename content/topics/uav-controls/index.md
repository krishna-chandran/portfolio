CINCINNATI — Krishna Chandran’s unmanned-air file is two plants and one habit: close the loops you can prove. A twelve-state quadrotor is asked to land on a truck that will not sit still. A small fixed wing is asked to hold airspeed, altitude and course. Both began as University of Cincinnati work under Prof. Dr. Rajnikanth Sharma. Both can be read without a MATLAB license.

This desk is the search door for **precision landing**, **quadrotor landing on a moving vehicle**, **gimbal geolocation**, and **fixed-wing UAV autopilot**.
