SOUTH SAN FRANCISCO — Krishnakumar Chandran works in artificial intelligence, physical AI, robotics and autonomous vehicles. The present job is Agtonomy, as a software engineer in data analytics: C++ on the tractor, Vector and AWS in the cloud, gRPC between the two. What separates the file from a typical software résumé is the seam: enterprise AI systems and machines that have to behave in a field, a factory or an apron.
<!--more-->

{{< well kind="lead" src="images/krishna.jpg" prompt="Portrait, 16:9, Cincinnati, natural window light, engineer in plain clothes, no grin for the camera." caption="Krishnakumar Chandran. Formal training in aerospace dynamics and controls; the rest of the paper is production." credit="Staff" />}}

He is from Erode, Tamil Nadu. Aeronautical engineering at Rajalakshmi Engineering College, Chennai, then Tata Consultancy Services in 2014 on ignio AIOps — network automation, event triage, incident prediction, automatic resolution. A master’s in aerospace at the University of Cincinnati (2017–2018) added UAVs, dynamics, controls and AI, and a Mazak-funded internship that put a spindle-health monitor on a Chicago show floor.

He returned to TCS in Farmington Hills as a product architect — ignio to ServiceNow, CyberArk and Veeva, a Kafka–ELK path that cut debug time by 90 percent. ThorDrive, 2021 to 2023, was the first long season in physical AI: FLIR, five cameras, two lidars, YOLO v4, and a platform for petabytes of ROS bags that raised capacity about 250 times. In 2023 he cofounded Protekk Web Technologies LLP and helped shape an education product around student-specific plans. In March 2024 he joined Agtonomy.
