SOUTH SAN FRANCISCO — On 12 March 2024 Krishnakumar Chandran joined Agtonomy as a software engineer in data analytics. The work is still running.
<!--more-->

{{< well kind="lead" prompt="Dusk, wide, 16:9: an autonomous tractor on a vineyard row, dust in the headlights, no people, documentary still." caption="Bobcat and Kubota machines in permanent crops — if the data arrives." credit="Staff" />}}

**The duties.** Custom C++ on the tractor; Docker; pipelines on Vector, InfluxDB, Timestream, OpenSearch, S3, ClickHouse and Kinesis. gRPC, Protobuf, sockets and REST. A vehicle-state agent that writes snapshots to Redis. Research the data tools. Design highly available vehicle-to-cloud telemetry. Land time series in databases, warehouses and lakes. Deploy Vector on Fargate and ECS with autoscaling and ELK monitoring. Migrate InfluxDB to Timestream, InfluxDB to S3, S3 to ClickHouse, with custom ETL.

**How it was done.** Three pipes: telemetry, customer metrics, interrupts. Architecture for as many as 300 vehicles. A key part in taking a fleet on the order of twenty machines toward 250, across Bobcat AT and CT and the Kubota M5N. Series B, a Kubota partnership, CES and FIRA sat in the same season.
