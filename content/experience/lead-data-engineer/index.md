CINCINNATI — While he held the estimation card at ThorDrive, Krishnakumar Chandran designed the internal big-data platform for ROS bags, live telemetry and operator logs. Field testing still meant a person on the apron. The career file says he built the infrastructure himself.

The platform later called Eitri collected petabytes, raised capacity by about 250 times, and added search, visualization, snapshot, parse, label, query, clip and download. Data-workflow time improved by 91.4 percent. An ELK path against the vehicle computers cut crashes by 90 percent. The conventional loop became a cloud query. Productivity, on that file, rose by more than 90 percent.

The stack sat on AWS with Elasticsearch, Logstash, Kibana, Beats, Postgres, Foxglove, Webviz and a React face. Once the tractor left the lot, the data path was the product.
