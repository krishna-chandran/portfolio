CINCINNATI — While he held the estimation card at ThorDrive, Krishnakumar Chandran designed the internal big-data platform for ROS bags, live telemetry and operator logs.
<!--more-->

{{< well kind="lead" prompt="Wide, 16:9, night shop: an airport tractor under work lights, lidar spinning, a laptop on the fender, documentary." caption="Find the clip. That was the product." credit="Staff" />}}

**The duties.** Collect and store petabytes. Raise capacity by about 250 times. Search, visualization, snapshot, parse, label, query, clip, download. ELK pipelines against the vehicle computers.

**How it was done.** The platform later called Eitri, on AWS. Data-workflow time improved by 91.4 percent. The ELK path cut vehicle crashes by 90 percent.
