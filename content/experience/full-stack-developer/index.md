CINCINNATI — On 22 July 2018 Krishnakumar Chandran joined the Intelligent Maintenance Systems laboratory as a full-stack engineer. Mazak funded the project. The appointment ended in December 2018.
<!--more-->

{{< well kind="lead" prompt="Wide, 16:9: a Mazak CNC in a university lab, vibration cable on the spindle, a monitor with a 0-100 health score, no people." caption="Below 50, service. Below 30, stop." credit="Staff" />}}

**The duties.** Research acquisition systems for CNC vibration. Integrate vibration and current sensors on an industrial PC. Store, network, preprocess, check quality. Extract and select features, classify healthy against faulty, emit a health value. Show logistic regression as a spindle score on a web page.

**How it was done.** FFT, PCA, logistic regression. Python and Flask to production. IMTS Chicago, September 2018. The university [wrote it up](https://www.uc.edu/news/articles/2019/01/n2063491.html) the following January.
