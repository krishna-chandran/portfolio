INDIA — In 2023 Krishnakumar Chandran cofounded Protekk Web Technologies LLP and sat with a client that wanted an education product, not a brochure.

**The duties.** Ideate the product from scratch. Design dynamic assessment across subjects. Build student-specific lesson plans. Put an AI recommendation system on the next exercise.

**How it was done.** The data, not the slogan, chose the path. The system was asked to evolve with demand, not only with the term calendar. A year of software for schools after a year of software for tractors. The habit was the same: observe, recommend, do not pretend the average child exists.
