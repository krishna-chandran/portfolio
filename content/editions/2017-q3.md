August 2017. The dateline changes. University of Cincinnati, aerospace, UAVs, dynamics, controls, AI. A fifty-percent graduate scholarship. The first project on the sheet is a fixed-wing autopilot — trim, PID, a state machine — flown in simulation before anyone trusts the air.

This is the edition in which the city becomes permanent. The later tractors are Midwestern machines. They start here.
