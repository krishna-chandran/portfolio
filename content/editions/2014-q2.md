The undergraduate years close on a concrete problem. The Tejas is developing play in a slat actuator after about three hundred runs. The assignment is to prove why, on paper, and to propose a member that spreads the load.

This edition is one story, told in full: the mathematics, the CATIA model, the ANSYS check, and the October journal note. It is the last quarter before software becomes the beat.
