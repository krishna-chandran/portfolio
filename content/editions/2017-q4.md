November fills the book. A quadcopter is asked to land on a moving truck. Path managers and trackers, geolocation, a tiltrotor EKF, a graph SLAM sketch. It is the thickest campus edition in the library.

Do not read it as a list of repositories. Read it as one autumn: a control student proving, in MATLAB and Gazebo, that he can close a loop on a vehicle that moves.
