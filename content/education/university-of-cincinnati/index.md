CINCINNATI — In August 2017 Krishnakumar Chandran enrolled for a Master of Engineering in aerospace at the University of Cincinnati. He finished in December 2018 with a GPA of 3.5 on 4.0, on a 50 percent graduate scholarship.
<!--more-->

The focus was unmanned air systems, analytical dynamics, modern control and artificial intelligence, with decision engineering and intelligent data analysis on the side. The Mazak spindle project sat inside that year. In 2019 he placed as second runner-up at IQ E-Pitch. He did not leave the city.
